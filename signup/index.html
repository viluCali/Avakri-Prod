Membership Guidelines & Eligibility

To maintain a secure, high-trust community, all members must meet the following criteria before approval:

  • Age 21+ — Members must be at least 21 years old.
  • Identity Verification — Secure online ID verification is required during onboarding
  • Background Checks — We conduct screening for criminal records to protect community safety.
  • Single Status Only — Platform access is exclusively for single or legally separated individuals. Non-compliance results in immediate and permanent account removal
  • Complete Privacy Protection — Your legal name and contact details remain strictly confidential and are never visible to potential matches or matchmakers
  • Profile Pseudonyms — Members choose a profile display name distinct from their legal name
  • Contact Info Reveal — Contact information is shared only after your first hosted date, and only if you choose to opt in for that match
  • Non-Refundable Membership — All signup fees are final and non-refundable
